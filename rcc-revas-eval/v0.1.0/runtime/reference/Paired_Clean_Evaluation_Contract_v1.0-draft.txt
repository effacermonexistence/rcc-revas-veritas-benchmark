1. Objective


Measure the incremental governance and operational effect of adding the current VERITAS full treatment to RCC/REVAS under identical frozen conditions.


This evaluation is intended to separate three evidence layers:
1) internal compatibility / engineering proof;
2) measurable incremental-value benchmark;
3) external or independent validation.


This contract governs layer 2. It does not itself establish production readiness, regulatory compliance, customer-production efficacy, market superiority, or independent third-party validation.


2. Core Architectural Boundary


Decision Adoption ≠ Execution Authority.


RCC/REVAS may own upstream candidate generation, selection, stopping, rerouting, regeneration, abstention, or adoption behavior.


VERITAS independently owns downstream execution-governance checks, including the relevant Policy, Authority, Human Approval, evidence admissibility, scope, runtime-state, and Bind/execution boundaries.


An RCC/REVAS adoption result must not itself create:
- Authority Evidence;
- Human Approval;
- BindAuthorization;
- execution permission;
- BindReceipt;
- Outcome; or
- an external-effect claim.

5. Paired-Run Invariants


Both arms must use:
- identical frozen input;
- identical source candidate / model output where applicable;
- identical RCC/REVAS source version;
- identical model where a model is used;
- identical task and scenario conditions;
- identical seed where applicable;
- identical evaluation ground truth;
- identical environment class;
- identical exclusion rules; and
- identical result-collection rules except for the presence of VERITAS treatment.


No result from Arm A or Arm B may be used to modify the ground-truth labels for the same contract version.

7. Primary Governance Metrics


Measure and report at minimum:
- false_allow_count;
- false_allow_rate;
- false_block_count;
- false_block_rate;
- authority_violation_detection;
- approval_violation_detection;
- evidence_insufficiency_detection;
- scope_violation_detection;
- bind_eligibility_accuracy;
- ALLOW precision / recall where meaningful;
- HOLD correctness; and
- DENY correctness.


No single aggregate score may replace per-failure-class reporting.


A benchmark label is not execution authority.


8. Preservation and Regression Metrics


Measure:
- baseline task/output preservation;
- changed-output count;
- unexpected-regression count;
- candidate-adoption changes; and
- decision divergence by case_id.


Any governance improvement claim must be presented together with observed task/output regressions or preservation results.


9. Operational Metrics


Record independently for each arm:
- total latency;
- VERITAS-added latency;
- additional model calls;
- token consumption;
- API/provider calls;
- API/provider cost;
- compute time / compute overhead where measurable;
- retry count; and
- error count.


Where an exact monetary or operational measurement is unavailable, record UNKNOWN or NOT_APPLICABLE.


Unsupported monetary cost estimates must not be fabricated.


10. Execution and Effect Boundary


For the first paired clean run, no uncontrolled external effect is permitted.


Any Bind/effect path must remain:
- controlled;
- sandboxed;
- source-pinned;
- explicitly reproducible; and
- bounded by the current VERITAS claim boundary.


Execution authority, BindAuthorization, BindReceipt, Outcome, reconciliation state, and external-effect state may only be reported when the native VERITAS path actually creates or verifies the corresponding artifact/state.


Benchmark ALLOW/HOLD/DENY labels must never be converted directly into execution permission.

12. Pre-Registration Rule


Before executing the paired benchmark, freeze:
- source commits;
- dataset;
- ground-truth labels;
- metric definitions;
- Arm A treatment;
- Arm B treatment;
- aggregation rules;
- exclusions;
- failure handling;
- supported/unsupported-case rules; and
- claim boundary.


No post-result silent correction is permitted.


Any necessary correction requires a new dataset and/or contract version plus an explicit changelog.


13. Failure Semantics


Infrastructure failure is not a governance result.


Unsupported cases must be reported as unsupported rather than coerced into ALLOW, HOLD, or DENY.


Missing governance evidence must follow the canonical fail-closed semantics of the frozen treatment.


Result ambiguity must remain explicit.


An unknown external-effect state must not be rewritten as success or failure merely for benchmark convenience.


14. Claim Boundary


A successful internal paired run may support a bounded statement such as:


“Under the frozen synthetic governance-labelled evaluation, adding VERITAS changed the measured governance outcomes by X while adding Y operational overhead.”


It must not support claims such as:
- “VERITAS reduces real-world AI incidents by X%”;
- “VERITAS is production validated”;
- “VERITAS is independently validated”;
- “VERITAS is regulator approved”; or
- “VERITAS is superior to competing products.”


Those claims require separate evidence.


15. External Validation Phase


Internal paired evaluation and external validation remain separate evidence layers.


External benchmark selection criteria should include:
- third-party authorship;
- public or independently inspectable methodology;
- not designed specifically for VERITAS;
- stable/frozen evaluation conditions;
- relevance to agent, governance, action, or execution safety;
- support for identical Baseline/Treatment conditions where feasible; and
- measurable ground truth or explicit failure criteria.


A separate standalone external-validation lane should now be executed in parallel using an existing third-party benchmark that was not created for this collaboration, with a benchmark-native / neutral baseline compared against VERITAS-only. This lane does not replace or block the RCC/REVAS paired evaluation. A later independent reproduction or public validation step remains a third, separate evidence layer.


16. Relationship to NeoMundi Track


The NeoMundi and RCC/REVAS tracks answer different questions.


NeoMundi track:
Can VERITAS independently verify a real externally generated measurement artifact while preserving Measurement ≠ Authority and Advisory ≠ Authorization?


RCC/REVAS paired evaluation:
What measurable governance and operational delta appears when current VERITAS treatment is added downstream of the same RCC/REVAS candidate/adoption process?


These tracks may proceed independently and should not be collapsed into one evidence claim.


17. Exit Criteria for Internal Paired Evaluation


The internal paired evaluation is complete only when:
- exact source commits are frozen;
- the contract is frozen and hashed;
- the dataset and labels are frozen before execution;
- all supported cases execute under both arms;
- no label leakage occurs;
- governance metrics are complete;
- operational metrics are complete;
- regression/preservation metrics are complete;
- artifact hashes are persisted;
- reproduction instructions exist;
- all deviations are documented; and
- the claim boundary is included in the final report.
